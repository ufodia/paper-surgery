# Code of conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and specific. Welcome people with different backgrounds and skill levels. Critique ideas and behavior without personal attacks. Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, and sharing private data are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation proportionately. Raise moderation concerns through the profile contact route at [Ufodia](https://github.com/ufodia), requesting a private channel if needed. Maintainers avoid publishing personal details about a report without consent.

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project-managed spaces.
